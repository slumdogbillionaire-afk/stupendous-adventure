HOW TO PLAY

THE GENIE'S DEAL

Every hero asked a genie for a power. The genie gave them what they asked for — AND the equal-and-opposite power they didn't ask for. Both sides are real powers.

GOAL

Be the last player standing! Every player starts with 100 HP.

SETUP

Each player picks a Champion (with a unique ability) and is dealt 7 cards. Cards may be heroes OR action cards.

ON YOUR TURN

  • DRAW a card from the deck.
  • HERO? Keep them (max 8) or discard.
  • ACTION? Hold for later or use immediately.
  • BATTLE ⚔ — pick an enemy hero, fight, winner takes the loser's card.
  • Use any held action cards from your team panel anytime on your turn.

ACTION CARDS

Mixed into the deck. Steal heroes, banish them, swap, heal, damage, recover from discard pile, shield yourself, or unleash chaos.

DISCARD PILE

Defeated heroes and discarded cards go here. RAID THE GRAVEYARD action cards can pull them back.

OVERKILL 💥

Battle damage past a hero's 0 HP spills onto the losing player's HP.

REVIVE ✨

If you have Hyper Heal, The Rewind, or Dream Weaver in your team and your HP hits 0, auto-revive at 20 HP. Or hold a SECOND WIND action card for 60 HP. One revive per game.

WINNING

Last player with HP wins!
